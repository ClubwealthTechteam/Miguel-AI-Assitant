Workflow Process Form

A universal form for documenting the work you do by hand, so we can find where AI and automation can take it off your plate.

Purpose of the form

This form is designed to help identify manual, repetitive, time-consuming, and complex work processes within each department. The information collected will be used to understand how tasks are currently performed, what tools and systems are involved, where bottlenecks occur, and which steps can potentially be improved through AI and automation.

The goal is to reduce manual work, simplify complex processes, eliminate unnecessary repetitive tasks, improve workflow efficiency, and create smarter systems while keeping appropriate human review and decision-making where needed.

Understanding the workaround

Please answer the questions below based on one of your regular or repetitive work processes. The goal is to understand the current workflow, identify bottlenecks, and determine where AI or automation could help.

Important: Please be specific about the process and how you use each app. "I update the CRM" tells us little. "I copy the applicant's phone number from the Jotform email into Recruit CRM and change their stage to Screening" tells us exactly what can be automated.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back.

How to fill out this form

Read the full SOP with a worked example See an example map
  1. Pick your tasks: the ones you do often by hand. You can document several in this form using "+ Add another task".
  2. List your tools in Q1: every web tool, app and named spreadsheet the process touches.
  3. Describe, then map it in Q2: one box per action in one tool. Use the plus above / below a step for YES / NO decisions.
  4. Add the details in Q3–Q6: information needed, repetitive steps with time spent, decisions, and bottlenecks.
  5. Map the ideal version in Q7: how it would run if it ran itself, and where you'd still approve.
  6. Choose your preference in Q8 (your own opinion), type ACKNOWLEDGED, and submit.
