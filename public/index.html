Workflow Process Form

Three short questions about the tasks you do by hand, so we can find where AI and automation can take it off your plate.

What this form is for

Tell us about all the tasks you listed in your 10 bullet points in the AI audit. We'll use your answers to find where AI and automation can take the work off your plate.

  1. Summarize each task in a few sentences.
  2. Map how you do each one today. Use "+ Add another task" so every task gets its own map.
  3. Optional: show how you wish they worked.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back.

Your details

01

Process summary

Go through each of your 10 tasks. For each one, in a few sentences: what the task is, what starts it, how often you do it, and about how long it takes. Number them 1–10 so they match your maps below.

02

Manual process How you do it today

Make one map per task: give it the task's title, then click + Add another task for the next one. Map each step as Tool → What you do in it, in the order you do it. Use the plus above a step for a YES path and below for a NO path when the process splits on a decision.

03

Ideal automation Optional

Your ideal vision only, no wrong answers. How would this task run if it did itself, and where would you still check or approve? If you don't know the tool, write "Automatically".

What happens next

Once this form is completed, please expect to be invited to Room 10 via the CW Helpdesk Zoom meeting with Miguel and Camille. You will be asked to show how you do the process, and Miguel may sit in for a shadow session.
